Connect the Bitunix Telegram group

  1. On this PC: get TELEGRAM_API_ID + TELEGRAM_API_HASH from https://my.telegram.org (API development tools). Put them in .env.
  2. Run python serve.py and open this desk. Click Show QR.
  3. On your phone, open Telegram → Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device, and scan the QR. Use the account that is already in the Bitunix group.
  4. Click Load my groups, pick the Bitunix futures group, then Watch this group. New posts land in the inbox and get Binance risk plans.
